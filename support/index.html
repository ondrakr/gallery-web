Frequently Asked Questions

Does Gallery Swiper delete photos permanently?

Photos are moved to your device's "Recently Deleted" album, following the standard iOS Photos app behavior, and are permanently removed after the usual retention period (or when you empty that album manually).

Does the app store or upload my photos anywhere?

No. Gallery Swiper works entirely on your device and never uploads or stores your photos outside of your device's Photos library.
