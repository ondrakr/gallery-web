Privacy Policy - Gallery Swiper

Last updated: 2026-07-02

Gallery Swiper is a fully local application. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user content.

Photo Library Access

Gallery Swiper requests access to your device's photo library solely to let you browse and delete photos and videos directly on your device. All actions happen locally - nothing is uploaded, copied, or sent anywhere.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data. Gallery Swiper does not use analytics, tracking, advertising SDKs, or any third-party services.

Data Storage

No data leaves your device. Gallery Swiper does not have a server or backend.
